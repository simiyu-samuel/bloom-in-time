Proposed dates use Africa/Nairobi time. Daily availability is unknown; these are scheduling targets rather than effort promises. The gates determine readiness.

## Milestone gates

G1 Mechanic: one complete two-flower puzzle; visible equal/unequal arrivals; deterministic evaluation; no hand-only dead ends.

G2 Content: all authored levels validate and solve; a player can complete and resume the progression.

G3 Experience: a first bloom is understandable; hints and timing cues work without sound; pause and input loss recover.

G4 Release: clean production build, public application link, checked video, accurate form, licensed assets, recorded commit.

If G1 is still unresolved on Nov 5, simplify mechanics before adding content. If G2 is late, prioritize a shorter verified campaign and update claims. After Nov 13, prefer fixes and clarity over new features.

## First prototype decision

Observe whether players understand delayed arrival and enjoy adjusting routes. If it feels like waiting rather than reasoning, adjust pulse speed, visual explanation, or the puzzle layout before committing to full art.
