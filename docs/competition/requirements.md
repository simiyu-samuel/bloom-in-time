## Judging alignment

Innovation: synchronization plus a growing musical sculpture. Experience: understandable first bloom, self-paced puzzles, recoverable mistakes. Technical: deterministic timing and clean input/lifecycle behavior. Presentation: cohesive art/audio and honest footage. The four categories are equally weighted.

Best First Five Minutes is a relevant craft target. Awards are not guaranteed, and an entry is eligible for only one prize under the current FAQ.
